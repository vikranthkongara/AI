## 2. XCENA Raises $135M Betting Memory Is AI's Real Bottleneck

**Source:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/29/xcena-secures-135m-at-570m-valuation-betting-on-memory-as-ais-real-bottleneck/

Korean-American chip startup XCENA secured $135M Series B at $570M valuation for its MX1 chip that puts thousands of RISC-V cores directly adjacent to DRAM via CXL interconnect. The company claims its approach can reduce inference server requirements from 10 machines to one by eliminating data round-trips between GPUs and memory. Mass production on Samsung foundry lines is scheduled for end of 2026, with revenue expected in 2027.

**Why it matters:** As inference scales to trillions of daily queries, memory bandwidth -- not GPU FLOPS -- is becoming the binding constraint, and XCENA's architecture could fundamentally change data center economics.
