# AI Daily Digest

Automated daily summary of the top 10 AI happenings in the world — market, industry, research, and product launches.

Generated every day at 8:00 AM CST and pushed to this repo automatically.

## Format

Each file is named `MM-DD-YY.md` (e.g., `05-24-26.md`) and contains:

- Top 10 AI news/developments for that day
- Source links for each item
- Short plain-English intro for each story
- Easy to scan in under 5 minutes

## Categories Covered

- Model releases & breakthroughs
- Industry moves (acquisitions, partnerships, funding)
- Product launches & updates
- Research papers worth knowing
- Regulation & policy
- Open source highlights
- Enterprise AI adoption
- AI infrastructure & compute
- AI safety & ethics
- Market trends & data

## How It Works

A MeshClaw cron job runs daily at 8:00 AM CST:
1. Searches AI news sources for the day's top stories
2. Curates the top 10 most impactful items
3. Writes a summary with links
4. Commits and pushes to this repo
