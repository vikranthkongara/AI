## 2. Trump Signs Narrower Executive Order on AI Oversight After Industry Objections

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/trump-signs-narrower-executive-order-on-ai-oversight-after-industry-objections/)

President Trump signed an executive order establishing a voluntary 30-day government review window for powerful AI models before public release, down from the originally proposed 90-day window after industry pushback from figures like David Sacks. **Why it matters:** This sets the U.S. government's official posture on AI regulation as voluntary and industry-friendly, signaling that mandatory pre-release requirements are off the table for now.

## 3. Publishers Will Be Able to Opt Out of AI Search, Thanks to New UK Regulation

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/03/publishers-will-be-able-to-opt-out-of-ai-search-thanks-to-new-regulation/)

The UK's Competition and Markets Authority has ordered Google to provide publishers with a toggle to opt out of AI-powered search features including AI Overviews (2.5 billion monthly users) and AI Mode (over 1 billion monthly users). Google will test the opt-out with UK publishers first before rolling out globally. The CMA calls this a "world first" in restoring publisher control over content used in generative AI.

**Why it matters:** This is the first regulatory mandate giving content creators direct control over whether their work feeds AI search products, potentially reshaping the economics of AI-powered search if widely adopted.

## 9. Google Rolls Out Fake Call Detection to Protect Against AI Deepfake Scams

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/google-rolls-out-fake-call-detection-to-protect-against-ai-deepfake-impersonation-scams/)

Google launched a fake call detection feature for Android that uses a "digital handshake between devices" to verify calls are genuinely originating from the displayed contact, combating AI deepfake voice impersonation scams. The feature is built on RCS, enabled by default, and rolling out globally to Android 12+ devices starting with Pixel phones. When spoofing is detected, the system warns the recipient to hang up.

**Why it matters:** As AI-generated voice deepfakes become increasingly sophisticated, this is the first major platform-level defense deployed at scale to protect billions of phone users from impersonation fraud.
