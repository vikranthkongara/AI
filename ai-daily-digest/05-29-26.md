## 7. XCENA Raises $135M Betting Memory Is AI's Real Bottleneck

**Source:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/29/xcena-secures-135m-at-570m-valuation-betting-on-memory-as-ais-real-bottleneck/

South Korean chip startup XCENA raised $135M (Series B) at a $570M valuation for its MX1 chip, which places thousands of RISC-V cores directly adjacent to DRAM and connects via CXL (Compute Express Link). Founded by Samsung/SK Hynix veterans, the company claims what previously required 10 servers could run on one. Mass production chips are scheduled off Samsung's foundry lines by end of 2026.

**Why it matters:** As inference workloads explode, memory bandwidth — not raw GPU compute — increasingly becomes the performance ceiling; XCENA's approach of bringing compute to the data could unlock major efficiency gains for hyperscalers.
