## 6. Memory Now Two-Thirds of AI Chip Costs

**Source:** [Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/ai-chip-component-cost-shares)

Analysis from Epoch AI shows memory has grown to nearly two-thirds of AI chip component costs, up from under half just two years ago. As models get larger and context windows expand, HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) is becoming the bottleneck — and the expense.

**Why it matters:** The AI cost story is shifting from compute to memory. Teams optimizing for cost should focus on memory-efficient architectures, caching, and context management.
