## 1. DuckDuckGo Launches Browser Extensions for AI-Free Search as Traffic Surges 3x

**Source:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/duckduckgo-makes-its-no-ai-search-engine-easier-to-access-as-its-traffic-booms/

DuckDuckGo released Chrome and Firefox extensions that set its AI-free search page (noai.duckduckgo.com) as the default search engine. Traffic to the no-AI search option tripled on May 28 after Google announced an AI-first overhaul of its search engine. Ironically, DuckDuckGo still offers its own AI chatbot — it's the forced AI results in organic search that users are rejecting.

**Why it matters:** The first major product to monetize anti-AI sentiment signals a growing market segment that prefers traditional search without AI-generated answers.

## 2. NVIDIA Unveils Cosmos 3 — First Omni-Model for Physical AI

**Source:** https://nvidia.com/en-us/ai/cosmos/

NVIDIA launched Cosmos 3, described as "the first omni-model with native reasoning, world and action generation" built on a Mixture-of-Transformers architecture. Unlike previous versions that separated perception and generation, Cosmos 3 generates across text, image, video, sound, and action modalities natively. Key applications include robot policy models, physics-grounded world simulation, and synthetic video data for training autonomous systems.

**Why it matters:** This bridges the gap between language AI and physical-world AI, giving robotics and autonomous vehicle developers a unified foundation model instead of stitching together separate systems.

## 3. SoftBank Commits €75 Billion to French Data Center Buildout

**Source:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/30/softbank-says-it-will-invest-up-to-e75-billion-to-build-french-data-centers/

SoftBank announced plans to invest up to €75 billion (~$87B) to build data centers in France with up to 5 gigawatts of additional capacity. The first phase targets 3.1 GW in the Hauts-de-France region by 2031, with facilities in Dunkirk, Bosquel, and Bouchain. France's economic minister called it a testament to Macron's ambition to position France as a leading AI value chain destination.

**Why it matters:** This is one of the largest single-country AI infrastructure commitments ever, signaling Europe's aggressive bid to compete with the US and Middle East for AI compute sovereignty.

**Source:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/30/what-a-joke-github-copilots-new-token-based-billing-spurs-consternation-among-devs/

## 7. Developers Increasingly Refuse to Work Without AI Tools — Studies Question Productivity Gains

**Source:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/29/coders-are-refusing-to-work-without-ai-and-that-could-come-back-to-bite-them/

Developers have become so dependent on AI coding tools that they refuse to work without them, even for research studies. However, multiple studies suggest AI-generated code doesn't reliably improve productivity — METR's 2025 research found AI actually slowed developers down, and CodeRabbit found AI produces 1.7x more problems than human code. Companies reportedly spend 44% of their AI tokens fixing AI-generated bugs.

**Why it matters:** The gap between perceived and measured AI productivity raises serious questions about whether the current wave of AI coding tool adoption is creating technical debt faster than it's reducing development time.
