# Growth Indicators

Track engineers taking on new areas, mentoring others, leading design reviews, expanding scope, and demonstrating readiness for next-level responsibilities.

## Data Sources
- **CRUX CRs**: CRs in new packages/services (expanding scope), CRs reviewing junior engineers' work
- **Taskei**: Tickets in new areas, design task ownership, complexity of assigned work
- **Calendar**: Design review meetings led, mentoring sessions, interview participation
- **Slack**: Helping others in channels, answering questions, sharing knowledge
- **Email (Outlook)**: Cross-team collaboration threads, architecture discussions

## Instructions

1. **Track scope expansion** per engineer:
   - CRs authored in packages/services they haven't touched before
   - Taskei tickets in new technical areas (new language, new system, new domain)
   - Taking ownership of a new service or component
   - Engaging with unfamiliar codebases (first-time reviewer for a package)
   - Working across team boundaries

2. **Track mentoring and teaching**:
   - CR reviews given to junior engineers (detailed, educational comments)
   - Pairing sessions scheduled (from calendar)
   - Onboarding buddy assignments and engagement
   - Knowledge sharing sessions delivered (brown bags, tech talks)
   - Documentation written for others' benefit
   - Slack messages helping others troubleshoot

3. **Track leadership signals**:
   - Design documents authored
   - Design reviews led (from calendar invites they organized)
   - Technical decisions documented and communicated
   - Architecture proposals or RFCs authored
   - Leading sprint planning or backlog grooming
   - Representing team in cross-team meetings

4. **Track increasing complexity**:
   - Story point/complexity trend of assigned tickets (taking on bigger items)
   - Multi-service changes (coordination across systems)
   - Performance or scalability work (typically senior-level)
   - Security-sensitive changes
   - Production debugging and incident leading

5. **Track collaboration breadth**:
   - Number of unique teams collaborated with
   - Cross-team CRs reviewed or authored
   - Participation in org-wide initiatives
   - Customer-facing discussions or presentations
   - Interview participation (demonstrates trusted judgment)

6. **Compare to level expectations**:
   - Current level responsibilities vs observed behaviors
   - Next-level behaviors being demonstrated
   - Areas where growth is needed for promotion readiness
   - Timeline of growth trajectory
