# End of Day Recap

Run my end-of-day summary. Do ALL of the following:

## 1. What Happened Today

Summarize today's activity:
- Emails I received that required action — which ones did I respond to vs. still pending
- Slack DMs received today — any unanswered threads
- Meetings I had today — list them with times

## 2. Open Action Items

Compile anything still outstanding:
- Emails needing my reply
- Slack DMs I haven't responded to
- CRs waiting on my review
- Taskei items assigned to me that are overdue or due today
- Any approvals or decisions pending from me

## 3. Tomorrow Preview

Check tomorrow's calendar:
- List all meetings with times and attendees
- Flag any conflicts or back-to-back blocks with no break
- For 1:1s, note any prep I should do tonight

## 4. Escalations & Risks

Flag anything that could blow up overnight:
- Active incidents or SEVs involving my team
- Tickets approaching SLA deadlines
- Deployments in progress or scheduled overnight
- Blocked team members who are waiting on me

Send the full recap as a single consolidated Slack DM formatted for quick scanning. Use sections with headers and bullet points. End with a "Top 3 things to handle first tomorrow" list.
