# Promotion Document

Compile a promotion document with role context, impact metrics, leadership principles evidence, peer feedback, and growth trajectory.

## Data Sources
- Internal search: level expectations guide, leadership principles definitions, org promotion calibration criteria
- Taskei: projects delivered, scope of ownership, complexity of work items
- Pipelines/Apollo: systems owned, operational metrics for those systems, improvements delivered
- CRs: code review activity (authored, reviewed), design contributions
- Oncall: operational excellence contributions, incidents managed
- Email/Slack: cross-team collaboration evidence, mentorship examples, stakeholder feedback
- Calendar: presentations given, design reviews led, interviews conducted

## Instructions
1. **Role and level context (1 paragraph).** Current role/level, target role/level, time in current role. Brief statement of the promotion thesis: why this person, why now.
2. **Scope demonstration (1-2 paragraphs).** Show that the candidate is consistently operating at the next level's scope. Use specific examples of decisions made, systems designed, teams influenced, ambiguity navigated.
3. **Impact metrics (1-2 paragraphs).** Quantified business impact of the candidate's contributions over the past 12-18 months. Revenue, cost savings, efficiency gains, customer impact -- with specific numbers and attribution.
4. **Leadership Principles evidence.** Select 6-8 most relevant LPs. For each, provide 2-3 specific examples:
   - Situation: what was the context (1 sentence)
   - Action: what did the candidate do (1-2 sentences)
   - Result: what was the measurable outcome (1 sentence)
   - LP connection: how this demonstrates next-level behavior for this principle
5. **Peer feedback summary (1 paragraph).** Synthesize feedback from 4-6 peers (cross-team, reports, skip-level). Identify themes. Note any growth areas mentioned and how they have been addressed.
6. **Growth trajectory (1 paragraph).** How has this person grown over the past 12-18 months? What challenges did they overcome? How has their scope expanded? What does their trajectory suggest about future impact?
7. **Risks and mitigations.** Acknowledge any potential concerns a reviewer might raise and address them proactively with evidence.

### Amazon Writing Style Rules
- Data-driven: "Led migration of 47 services to new platform, reducing deployment time from 45 minutes to 8 minutes" not "led an important migration"
- No weasel words: "consistently high performer" must become "delivered all 6 committed OKRs at 0.8+ score for 3 consecutive quarters"
- Specificity: name the projects, the systems, the metrics, the dates
- Customer-obsessed: frame impact in terms of customer outcomes, not just internal achievements
- Evidence over assertion: every claim must be backed by a specific example or metric
- "So what" for every example: connect it to why it demonstrates next-level behavior

## Output Format
```
[Title: Promotion Document - <Candidate Name>]
[Current: <Level/Role>] [Proposed: <Level/Role>]
[Manager: <name>] [Date: <date>]

Promotion Thesis
[1 paragraph: why this person, why now]

Scope and Impact
[2-3 paragraphs on scope at next level and quantified impact]

Leadership Principles

[LP 1: <Name>]
Example 1: [Situation / Action / Result]
Example 2: [Situation / Action / Result]

[LP 2: <Name>]
...

[Continue for 6-8 LPs]

Peer Feedback
[1 paragraph synthesizing themes]

Growth Trajectory
[1 paragraph on development arc]

Risks and Mitigations
[1 paragraph addressing potential concerns]

Appendix
[Metrics table, project list, feedback quotes, timeline of key contributions]
```

## Delivery
Save as draft document. Send Slack DM to the document owner with the link. Flag any sections where additional data or examples are needed.
