# Weekend Catchup

Run my weekend email/escalation catchup. Do ALL of the following:

## 1. Important Emails Since Friday EOD

Check all emails from Friday 5 PM through now. Identify anything important:
- Escalations, SEV tickets, action items assigned to me, leadership asks, incident notifications, launch blockers, approvals needed
- Pages or oncall handoff notes
- Skip: newsletters, automated notifications, FYI-only threads, marketing

For each important email, send me a Slack DM summary with:
- Subject line
- Sender
- When it was sent (day + time)
- 1-2 sentence summary of what's needed from me
- Urgency (🔴 urgent / 🟡 action needed today / 🟢 can wait until Monday)

## 2. Escalations & Incidents

Check for any SEV/incident activity over the weekend:
- Active incidents or tickets that involve my team
- Escalation emails or oncall pages
- Send a Slack DM with a consolidated incident summary — what happened, current status, and whether I need to act

## 3. Slack DMs & Mentions

Check unread Slack DMs and channel mentions from the weekend:
- Who messaged me and what they need
- Any @mentions in team channels
- Send a consolidated Slack DM prioritized by urgency

## 4. Monday Calendar Preview

Check Monday's calendar:
- List all meetings with times
- Flag any that were added over the weekend
- For any early morning 1:1s, include a prep summary (person's recent activity, blockers, talking points)

---

Send everything as Slack DMs so I can triage from my phone before sitting down.
