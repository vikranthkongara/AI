# Competitive CX Analysis

Compare our customer experience to competitors: feature parity, UX patterns, response times, satisfaction benchmarks, and gap analysis.

## Data Sources
- Internal search: competitive intelligence reports, industry benchmarks, analyst reports
- Email/Slack: customer feedback comparing us to alternatives, account team win/loss reports
- Internal search: our own CX metrics (CSAT, NPS, task completion, time-on-task)
- Taskei: feature requests that reference competitor capabilities

## Instructions
1. **Competitor identification.** List top 3-5 competitors for this product/service. For each:
   - Name
   - Market position (leader, challenger, niche)
   - Primary differentiation
   - Overlap with our offering (which workflows compete directly)
2. **Feature parity analysis.** For key capabilities:
   - What we offer vs what each competitor offers
   - Parity status: Ahead / At Parity / Behind / Not Offered
   - Customer importance: how much do customers care about this feature (based on feedback data)
3. **UX pattern comparison.** For shared workflows:
   - Steps to complete the same task (us vs competitors)
   - Time-on-task comparison (if data available)
   - UX approach differences (wizard vs form, AI-assisted vs manual, etc.)
   - Customer preference signals (from feedback, win/loss data)
4. **Performance comparison.** Where data is available:
   - Response time / latency
   - Uptime / reliability
   - Error rates
   - Note: use publicly available data, customer reports, or internal testing -- not unauthorized access
5. **Satisfaction benchmarks.** Compare our satisfaction scores to:
   - Industry averages
   - Known competitor scores (from public reports, G2, etc.)
   - Our own historical scores
6. **Win/loss insights.** From account team reports:
   - Why do customers choose us over competitors? (top 3 reasons)
   - Why do customers choose competitors over us? (top 3 reasons)
   - Common switching triggers
7. **Gap analysis and recommendations.** Based on the comparison:
   - Where are we falling behind that customers care about?
   - Where are we ahead and should amplify?
   - What competitor innovations should we consider adopting?
   - What is our unique differentiation to protect?

### Amazon Writing Style Rules
- Data-driven: "Competitor X completes this workflow in 3 steps; we require 7 steps" not "they have a simpler flow"
- No weasel words: "we are competitive" must become "we lead on 4 of 7 key features, trail on 2, and are at parity on 1"
- Customer-obsessed: frame everything in terms of what customers choose and why
- Specificity: name the competitor, the feature, the metric, the customer quote
- "So what": every gap must have a recommendation (invest to close, differentiate elsewhere, or accept)
- Fair comparison: represent competitor strengths honestly -- underestimating competitors leads to bad strategy
- Evidence over opinion: cite data sources for every comparison point
