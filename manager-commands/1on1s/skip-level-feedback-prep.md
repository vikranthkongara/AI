# Skip-Level Feedback Preparation

Compile feedback and talking points for my skip-level 1:1 with my manager, representing my team's concerns, achievements, and needs.

## Data Sources
- Taskei: Team accomplishments, blocked items needing leadership help, resource constraints
- CRUX CRs: Team delivery velocity, notable technical achievements
- Slack: Team sentiment signals, recurring frustrations raised, cross-team friction points
- Pipelines: Delivery milestones hit or missed, deployment health
- Oncall system: Operational burden on team, incident trends, escalation patterns
- Calendar: My meeting load, time spent on management vs. IC work, team ceremony health
- Email (Outlook): Escalations received, leadership communications relevant to team

## Instructions
1. **Team Wins to Highlight** (what my manager should know about):
   - Query Taskei for major milestones completed by my team in the last 2 weeks
   - Check Pipelines for successful launches
   - Identify individual contributors who deserve visibility at the skip level
   - Note any process improvements or innovations the team drove
2. **Concerns to Raise** (things I need help with):
   - Check Taskei for blocked items that need leadership intervention
   - Identify cross-team dependencies that are stuck and need escalation
   - Review oncall system for operational burden trends that indicate understaffing
   - Check Slack for recurring team frustrations I can't solve at my level
3. **Resource & Support Needs**:
   - Assess headcount needs based on workload distribution
   - Identify skill gaps on the team that need hiring or training investment
   - Note any tooling or infrastructure needs the team has raised
4. **Organizational Feedback** (from my team, anonymized):
   - Synthesize themes from recent 1:1s about org-level concerns
   - Note any policy or process friction affecting team productivity
   - Capture suggestions from the team about what leadership could do differently
5. **Recognition Requests**:
   - Identify team members who deserve recognition at a higher level
   - Prepare specific examples and impact for promotion discussions
   - Note any spot award or peer bonus nominations to discuss
6. **Questions to Ask My Manager**:
   - Upcoming org changes that affect my team
   - Strategic direction clarity needed
   - Feedback on my own performance and growth

## Output Format
```
## Skip-Level 1:1 Prep
### Meeting with: {{MANAGER_NAME}}
### Date: {{DATE}}

### Team Wins to Share (2 min)
1. **[Achievement]**: [Impact and who drove it]
2. **[Achievement]**: [Impact and who drove it]
- Recognition ask: [Person] deserves visibility for [specific contribution]

### Concerns & Blockers Needing Help (5 min)
1. **[Issue]**: [Context, impact, what I've tried, what I need from them]
2. **[Issue]**: [Context, impact, what I've tried, what I need from them]

### Resource Discussion (3 min)
- Current team utilization: [High/Moderate/Sustainable]
- Key gap: [Skill or headcount need]
- Proposal: [What I'm asking for and why]

### Team Sentiment (Anonymized) (3 min)
- Theme 1: [What the team is feeling about X]
- Theme 2: [What the team is feeling about Y]
- Suggested action: [What leadership could do]

### Questions for My Manager (5 min)
1. [Strategic question about upcoming changes]
2. [Clarity question about priorities]
3. [Feedback request for myself]

### FYI Items (Low Priority, Share if Time Permits)
- [Item that's informational but not urgent]
```

## Delivery
Send as a Slack DM to me 2 hours before my skip-level 1:1 (check calendar for the meeting time).
