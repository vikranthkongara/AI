# Manager Self-Review

Self-assessment of my effectiveness as a manager over the past month, using observable signals to identify where I'm supporting the team well and where I need to improve.

## Data Sources
- Slack: My response times to team members, frequency of proactive check-ins, availability signals
- Calendar: Time allocation (1:1s kept vs. cancelled, team meetings, focus time for team strategy)
- Taskei: Decisions I made or unblocked, tasks I own that are overdue, team blockers I resolved
- CRUX CRs: My review turnaround time, quality of architectural guidance in reviews
- Email (Outlook): Response times to team, escalation handling speed, communication to stakeholders
- Oncall system: How quickly I responded to team escalations, support during incidents

## Instructions
1. **Responsiveness Assessment**:
   - Measure my average response time to team Slack DMs in the last 30 days
   - Measure my average response time to emails from direct reports
   - Check how quickly I responded to oncall escalations
   - Identify any messages or requests that went unanswered for >24 hours
2. **1:1 Quality**:
   - Check calendar: How many 1:1s did I keep vs. cancel/reschedule in the last month?
   - Were 1:1s held at consistent cadence for all direct reports (no one neglected)?
   - Did I spend prep time before 1:1s (check for calendar blocks or notes)?
3. **Decision Making**:
   - Review Taskei for decisions I was asked to make. How quickly did I respond?
   - Identify any decisions still pending that are blocking the team
   - Check if I'm the bottleneck on any task or CR approvals
4. **Time Allocation**:
   - Analyze my calendar: What % of time went to each category?
     - Direct report support (1:1s, coaching, unblocking)
     - Team strategy and planning
     - Cross-team coordination
     - Hiring and interviews
     - Individual contributor work
     - Meetings that didn't need me
5. **Team Support Quality**:
   - Did I proactively check on team members during stressful periods (oncall, launches)?
   - Did I shield the team from unnecessary interruptions/requests?
   - Did I follow through on commitments I made in 1:1s?
6. **Communication Effectiveness**:
   - Did I share relevant context from leadership meetings with the team promptly?
   - Were my team communications clear and actionable?
   - Did I provide timely feedback (positive and constructive)?
7. **Growth Areas**:
   - Identify patterns where I fell short
   - Suggest specific improvements for next month
